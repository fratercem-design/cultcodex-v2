What you've taken on

The most important work no one sees

When you do this well, no one notices you did anything at all. A well-guarded space simply feels safe.

No one sees the troll removed before they could wound, or the conflict defused before it exploded. This is a labor of love, performed in the dark — and the whole community rests on it.

Who you are

The lantern, not the sword

You are not the community's police, its army, or its judge. You are a Lantern Bearer — carrying light to the edges so others can see and be safe.

One rules the community. The other serves it from the shadows. Choose the lantern, always — even when the sword would be easier.

The core discipline

Restraint

The power to remove and to silence is real power — and real power corrupts quietly. Intervene as little as possible, and as much as necessary.

Guard the edges. Do not police the center. An over-moderated space is as dead as an unmoderated one is dangerous.

The skill beneath the skills

Read the Room

By the time trouble is obvious, it is already late. Learn to feel it while it is still just a shift in the air.

Watchthe pace, the tone, the edges, the quiet ones, the recurring names.
Act early— a gentle nudge at the first flicker beats a ban at the peak.
When it heats up

De-escalation

Your first act is not on the room. It is on yourself — an agitated moderator pours fuel on the fire; a calm one starves it.

Slowthe pace — heat thrives on speed.
Lowerthe stakes — it's a disagreement, not a war.
Namethe shared ground beneath the clash.
Separatethe people from the problem — take it private when you can.
Know the difference

Good Faith & Bad Faith

Good faith → mediate

Sincere, if clumsy. Can state your view, updates when shown wrong, wants resolution. Meet with patience and questions.

Bad faith → contain

Moving goalposts, one-sided standards, seeking your reaction not resolution. Name it once, then bound it. Don't feed it.

Charity first — always. But to keep mediating bad faith is to let it burn the room while you reason with the arsonist.

Agree on this before you need it

The Escalation Ladder

1IgnoreFirst mild bait gets nothing. Starve it — no reaction from you or chat.
2One boundaryOne calm, authoritative line: “That's not it. Moving on.” Then never again.
3Time-out / muteQuiet removal, no drama, no public fight.
4BanRepeat or serious violation. No farewell speech, no debate.
5LockdownRaid: subscriber/slow mode, batch-ban, ride it out. It passes.
No nuance here

The Hard Line

Threats · Doxxing · Danger to a real person

This is not trolling — it is danger. Remove instantly. Screenshot for evidence. Report to the platform, and if credible, to authorities.

The moment a conflict moves toward someone's physical safety, we are no longer moderating a debate. There is no cause that justifies it.

The balance you carry

The Twin Guardianship

Protect free speech

The freedom to question, challenge, and say the uncomfortable thing. Attack ideas freely.

Protect human dignity

The right to take part without being degraded or dehumanized. Cruelty to a person, never.

Guard the content of speech generously; guard the conduct of speech firmly. This line never draws perfectly — draw it anyway, in good faith, forever.

Where your authority comes from

The Disciplines of Trust

Your power is not in the ban button. It is in the community's belief that you use it fairly.

Transparencythe rules are known; the reasoning is available when asked.
Documentationwrite it down — the record is the memory of the gate.
Consistencythe same rule for the friend and the stranger. This one costs the most.
And this matters most of all
