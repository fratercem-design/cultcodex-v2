What this is

A community for exploring truth, exposing deception, embracing mystery, and helping each other understand ourselves and the strange corners of human nature —

through open, honest conversation.

About that word

Yes, we say “cult.”

We keep the dangerous word on purpose — so the danger stays visible. Here it means not a hierarchy of followers, but a culture of inquiry.

You are free to disagree. Free to challenge. Free to leave. That freedom is the whole foundation.

“There are no followers here.
There are only fellow travelers.”

Some carry lanterns. Some carry questions. Some carry scars. We walk toward the unknown together — not to conquer it, but to be changed by it.

What we hold

Every mystery is welcome.

Every certainty is suspect.

Every question is sacred.

We kneel before no ideology. We swear loyalty to no dogma. We worship no leader.

How we gather

The Open Panel

The oldest human technology: a circle around a fire, where people face one another and ask, “What have you discovered?”

The skeptic and the mystic, the scientist and the tarot reader — every voice adds a piece to the mosaic. The goal is not agreement. It is exploration.
