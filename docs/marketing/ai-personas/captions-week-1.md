# Week 1: Captions and Hashtags

One post a day per account for 7 days, 21 posts in total. Day 1 is a short intro
post for each character, and Days 2–7 use the scripts in [`scripts.md`](./scripts.md).
This replaces the 2-a-day burst in the original plan: a new account gets more from
one post a day than from six posts in three days, and each video gets a full day
to collect views before the next one goes up.

## Rules for every post

- **Turn on the AI label.** Instagram: *Advanced settings → AI info*. TikTok: *More options →
  AI-generated content*. Every caption also ends with the disclosure line below. Don't drop it.
- **3–5 hashtags, at the end of the caption.** More than that looks spammy on both platforms now.
- **`[PULL: …]` in a caption** means the same real archive detail you put in the video. Copy it
  exactly. Never post a caption with the brackets still in it.
- **Same caption on Instagram and TikTok.** On TikTok, if it runs long, cut from the middle
  and keep the first line, the call to action and the disclosure line.
- **Post times:** 9pm for the Archivist and Madame Sulphur, 11pm for Nyx. Use the timezone where
  most of the Cult of Psyche audience is.

**Disclosure line (last line of every caption, all three characters):**
`🤖 AI character · Cult of Psyche fan archive, not official`
