# TROLLOPEDIA — The Living Archive of the Psycheverse

> Master brief v2, delivered by John (Psyche) 2026-07-22. Canonical vision for CultCodex.me.
> Prime directive: **never build "pages" — build a living knowledge graph.**

## Primary Mission

The official knowledge archive for the Cult of Psyche — the definitive encyclopedia of the
Psycheverse. Simultaneously: the memory of the community, the encyclopedia of every guest,
the history of every conflict, the archive of every livestream, the mythology of the Cult,
and the searchable brain behind CultCodex.me. Everything connects, everything searchable,
everything discoverable. The site should feel like opening a forbidden occult archive
documenting fifteen years of internet history.

## What is the Psycheverse

The complete ecosystem around Cult of Psyche, Psycheverse, and Psyche's Nightmares:
Open Panels, Fight Nights, debates, drama, alliances, communities, recurring guests,
internet subcultures, livestream history, inside jokes, memes, occult discussions,
conspiracy research, internet mysteries, character development, community lore.
Every episode contributes to an evolving shared mythology.

## Canon & Evidence Discipline

CultCodex is the official archive. Everything references canonical evidence: YouTube
videos, livestream transcripts, chat logs (when available), descriptions, comments (when
permitted), screenshots, community submissions, archived social media.

**Every claim receives citations, confidence levels, timestamps, and provenance.**
Unsupported accusations, criminal allegations, or defamatory claims must never be stated
as fact. Clearly distinguish documented evidence, opinions, satire, rumors, and unresolved
disputes.

## Archive Philosophy

Every episode is a historical document. Every guest is a character. Every conversation is
part of an ongoing story. Every drama becomes a timeline. Every meme becomes folklore.
Every quote becomes searchable. Every relationship becomes visualized. The archive should
feel alive.

Historical Significance, Relationship Changes, Suggested Related Episodes, Watch Video,

## Relationship Engine

Relationships evolve over time — never fixed. States: Friend, Former Friend, Ally,
Frequent Collaborator, Debate Rival, Enemy, Occasional Guest, Moderator, Supporter,
Critic, Student, Mentor, Community Member, Unknown. Each relationship displays a timeline
showing how it changed across episodes.

## Psycheverse Timeline

Chronological community history: channel launches, first guest appearances, legendary
panels, major Fight Nights, community splits, reconciliations, platform bans, guest
returns, milestones, announcements, legal events (with reliable sourcing), projects,
collaborations. Every event links to supporting episodes and evidence.

## Lore Engine

Recurring ideas as mythology: The Cult, The Oracle, Nightmares, Pattern Recognition,
Occult Symbolism, Internet Archetypes, Digital Shamans, Signal vs Noise, Synchronicities,
Rabbit Holes, Timeline Shifts, Recurring Dreams, Community Rituals. Each lore page
references every episode where it appears.

## AI Features

Analyze every transcript to: identify guests, extract quotes, detect recurring topics,
generate summaries, build timelines, detect memes, identify recurring phrases, suggest
lore updates, update relationship graphs, find callback jokes, recommend similar episodes,
surface forgotten connections. Create "Previously on Cult of Psyche" summaries.

## Search

Search everything — people, quotes, topics, episodes, lore, guests, communities, Fight
Nights, debates, tarot, occult, books, symbols, catchphrases, memes, relationship types,
timeline events. Natural-language queries: "Show every episode where Guest A and Guest B
argued." "Find every discussion about remote viewing." "Which guest has appeared with
Psyche the most?" "Show all episodes mentioning Matangi."

## Rabbit Hole System

Every page encourages exploration: Appeared Together, Opposing Viewpoints, Most Similar
Guests, Shared Enemies, Shared Communities, Referenced In, Often Mentioned Alongside,
Episodes Before/After This, Most Related Lore. **Users should never reach a dead end.**

## Community Contributions

Trusted members can suggest edits, submit timestamps, upload screenshots, document lore,
correct metadata, tag episodes, create collections, vote on evidence quality. All edits
reviewed and versioned.

## Technical Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Redis, vector + semantic search,

comprehensive testing, accessibility-first.

## Development Process

Before coding: understand existing CultCodex architecture, review the current Prisma
schema, respect existing design tokens and components, avoid duplicate systems.
When improving: refactor instead of layering hacks, document architectural decisions,
create migration plans, write tests, review performance and security, preserve backward
compatibility where practical.

## Success Criteria

A visitor can spend hours exploring — from a guest, to a rivalry, to a Fight Night, to an
inside joke, to an occult topic, to another guest, to another era — without ever running
out of meaningful connections. The definitive historical archive of the Cult of Psyche,
preserved for viewers, researchers, documentarians, and future AI systems.
